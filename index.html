About QuadWatch

  • Drag a divider to resize. Double-click it to reset.
  • Press H to hide or show the top bar.
  • Edit videos lets you change, add or remove links.
  • Each video starts when you press play.

QuadWatch uses the YouTube embedded player (YouTube API Services) and is not affiliated with YouTube. By watching you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Your list of videos is kept only in this browser.
